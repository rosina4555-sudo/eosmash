# EOSMASH
## Waitlist Landing Page: UI/UX & Frontend Development Brief

**Document version:** 1.0  
**Project stage:** Pre-launch  
**Product:** Competitive skill-based gaming platform  
**Initial target market:** Ghana, with potential expansion across Africa  
**Primary objective:** Build anticipation, communicate the product clearly and collect qualified early-access registrations.

---

# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

## 1.1 About Eosmash

Eosmash is a competitive gaming platform being developed to connect players who want to challenge one another in skill-based video games.

Players will be able to select supported games, find opponents, compete in head-to-head matches and participate in organized competitions.

The planned business model includes competitions where eligible players contribute agreed entry stakes and compete for monetary prizes. The platform intends to earn revenue through a commission on completed competitions, subject to applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Eosmash aims to create an accessible, competitive gaming ecosystem where players can demonstrate their abilities, build their competitive reputation and participate in meaningful gaming challenges.

The initial market is Ghana, with potential expansion into other African markets.

## 1.2 Purpose of the website

This is a pre-launch landing page, not the full gaming platform.

Its primary purpose is to:

1. Introduce Eosmash to potential players.
2. Explain how the platform will work.
3. Establish a recognizable and professional brand.
4. Generate excitement around the upcoming launch.
5. Collect email addresses from interested gamers.
6. Identify the games and competition categories potential users prefer.
7. Build an initial audience before the product becomes available.

The website must make visitors understand the concept within a few seconds.

## 1.3 Target audience

The primary audience consists of adults who enjoy competitive video gaming.

Important audience segments:

- Football video game enthusiasts.
- Competitive mobile gamers.
- Racing game enthusiasts.
- Strategy and skill-game players.
- Gaming communities and university gaming groups.
- Players interested in organized gaming competitions.

The initial communication should feel particularly relevant to Ghanaian gamers without making the product look limited to Ghana.

# 2. BRAND IDENTITY AND VISUAL DIRECTION

## 2.1 Overall design direction

**Design concept: Premium competitive gaming.**

Eosmash should look like a modern gaming technology company with a strong competitive identity.

The design should combine:

- The sophistication of a modern SaaS product.
- The energy of competitive esports.
- The clarity of a premium consumer technology brand.
- The excitement of a competitive gaming environment.

It must NOT look like:

- An online casino.
- A sports betting website.
- A generic gaming blog.
- A traditional esports tournament website.
- A cheap gaming-themed template.
- An overly colourful arcade website.

The visual language should be confident, energetic, modern and restrained.

## 2.2 Brand personality

The brand should communicate these characteristics:

| Characteristic | Design interpretation |
|---|---|
| Competitive | Bold headings and strong visual hierarchy |
| Modern | Clean layouts and contemporary typography |
| Premium | Controlled colours and generous spacing |
| Energetic | Strong accent colour and purposeful motion |
| Trustworthy | Clear content, predictable interactions |
| Accessible | Simple language and intuitive navigation |

## 2.3 Colour system

The primary visual identity should use midnight navy and electric lime.

The lime colour represents energy, action, competition and achievement. The midnight background gives the platform a premium technology and esports appearance.

### Primary palette

| Colour | HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight | `#0B1020` | Primary page background |
| Deep Navy | `#111A2E` | Cards and secondary sections |
| Elevated Navy | `#17243B` | Inputs, panels and elevated surfaces |
| Electric Lime | `#C6F36A` | Primary CTA and highlights |
| Lime Hover | `#B3E653` | Hover and active CTA states |
| Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Main headings and important text |
| Soft White | `#E8EDF4` | Body text |
| Muted Slate | `#97A5B9` | Supporting text |
| Border | `#26344C` | Card borders and dividers |

### Supporting colours

| Colour | HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Success | `#54D69A` | Successful form submission |
| Error | `#FF7777` | Form validation errors |
| Warning | `#F4C66A` | Important notices |
| Subtle Accent | `#8B9BB5` | Secondary UI details |

### Colour usage rules

- Midnight should dominate the visual experience.
- Electric lime should be used selectively.
- Lime should primarily identify important actions and active states.
- Avoid using lime for large blocks of body text.
- Avoid using multiple bright accent colours simultaneously.
- Do not introduce random purple, pink or blue gradients.
- Avoid excessive glow effects and neon outlines.

The overall appearance must remain sophisticated rather than visually noisy.

## 2.4 Typography

Recommended typography:

**Headings: Space Grotesk**

- Modern geometric appearance.
- Strong visual presence.
- Suitable for competitive gaming branding.
- Use medium, semibold and bold weights.

**Body: Inter**

- Excellent readability.
- Clean appearance.
- Suitable for navigation, forms and supporting text.

Suggested type scale:

| Element | Desktop size | Weight |
|---|---:|---|
| Hero heading | 64–76px | 700 |
| Section headings | 40–48px | 650 |
| Card headings | 20–24px | 600 |
| Body text | 16–18px | 400 |
| Navigation | 14–15px | 500 |
| Supporting text | 13–14px | 400 |
| Button text | 14–16px | 600 |

Mobile hero heading should scale to approximately 40–48px.

Use a maximum line length that keeps paragraphs readable. Avoid excessively wide text blocks.

# 14. DESIGN SYSTEM AND COMPONENT SPECIFICATIONS

## 14.1 Layout

Maximum content width: 1200–1280px.

Desktop horizontal padding: 64–80px.

Tablet horizontal padding: 32–40px.

Mobile horizontal padding: 20–24px.

Recommended section spacing:

- Desktop: 100–120px vertical spacing.
- Tablet: 80–96px.
- Mobile: 64–80px.

Use consistent spacing throughout the page.

## 14.2 Cards

- Background: Deep Navy `#111A2E`.
- Border: `#26344C`.
- Border radius: 14–18px.
- Padding: 24–28px.
- Shadow: Minimal or none.
- Hover: Slight border emphasis and subtle background shift.

Avoid glassmorphism as a default styling technique.

## 14.3 Buttons

Primary button:

- Background: Electric Lime `#C6F36A`.
- Text: Midnight `#0B1020`.
- Font weight: 600.
- Height: 48–54px.
- Border radius: 10–12px.
- Hover: `#B3E653`.
- Focus: Visible lime/white focus ring.

Secondary button:

- Transparent background.
- Border: `#34445D`.
- Text: White.
- Same dimensions as primary.

Buttons should have clear hover, active, disabled and loading states.

## 14.4 Inputs

- Dark elevated background.
- Subtle border.
- White input text.
- Muted placeholder.
- Clear focus state.
- Minimum height: 48–52px.
- Accessible labels.
- Error text directly below the relevant input.

Do not rely on placeholders as the only input labels.

## 14.5 Icons

Use `lucide-vue-next` consistently.

Avoid mixing multiple icon libraries.

Use icons to support meaning, not as decoration everywhere.

## 14.6 Motion

Animation should be subtle and intentional.

Recommended:

- Smooth anchor scrolling.
- Small hover transitions.
- Fade-up section reveals.
- Gentle visual movement in the hero artwork.
- Button interaction feedback.
- Accordion transitions.

Motion duration: approximately 180–350ms for ordinary UI interactions.

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Avoid:

- Constant flashing.
- Excessive parallax.
- Large bouncing elements.
- Aggressive animated backgrounds.
- Animations that delay access to the waitlist form.

## Waitlist backend integration

The form should submit to a configurable backend endpoint.

Suggested payload:

```json
{
  "email": "player@example.com",
  "country": "Ghana",
  "preferred_game": "football",
  "device": "mobile"
}
```

Only the email is required.

The frontend must not assume the endpoint URL. It should be configurable through environment variables.

The backend should:

- Validate and normalize email addresses.
- Prevent duplicate registrations.
- Store optional preferences.
- Apply rate limiting.
- Return clear success and error responses.
- Avoid exposing private user information.

Suggested successful response:

```json
{
  "status": "success",
  "message": "You have joined the Eosmash waitlist."
}
```

Do not store email addresses in browser local storage as a substitute for backend persistence.

# 18. CONTENT AND CLAIMS POLICY

Accuracy is important because Eosmash is pre-launch.

Do not publish:

- Fake user counts.
- Fake testimonials.
- Fake tournament winners.
- Unconfirmed partnerships.
- Unsupported claims about anti-cheat systems.
- Guaranteed winnings.
- Unconfirmed launch dates.
- Game integrations that have not been secured.
- Claims that the platform is licensed or approved without evidence.

Do not describe Eosmash as an already operational cash gaming service.

The landing page must transparently communicate that monetary competitions are planned and will only be available where legally permitted.

Waitlist registration must not be presented as a deposit or a commitment to participate in monetary competitions.
